Question: Can I get LAMF against stocks or shares?
Simple answer: LAMF is a loan against mutual fund units only — not against individual stocks or equity shares. If you meant eligible mutual fund units (not stock market shares), LTV is up to 45% for equity mutual funds and up to 75% for debt mutual funds, based on the current market value of pledged units.
