SHRIRAM CREDIT — LAMF vs Stocks / Shares

Customer question: Can I get a loan against stocks?
Simple answer: No. Shriram Credit LAMF provides loans only against eligible mutual fund units — not against individual stocks or equity shares.

Customer question: Can I get a loan against my mutual fund stocks?
Simple answer: LAMF is a loan against mutual fund units only — not against individual stocks or equity shares. If you meant eligible mutual fund units (not stock market shares), LTV is up to 65% for equity mutual funds and up to 75% for debt mutual funds, based on the current market value of pledged units.

Customer question: Can I take a loan against equity shares or pledge shares?
Simple answer: No. We do not offer a loan against stocks or equity shares. This journey is only for Loan Against Mutual Funds — eligible mutual fund units shown in your application.

Customer question: Can I combine shares and mutual funds for a higher LAMF loan?
Simple answer: No. Only eligible mutual fund units shown in the LAMF journey can be considered. Shares and stocks cannot be used as collateral for this product.
