SHRIRAM CREDIT — LAMF Additional Pledge (Not Available)

Customer question: How can I do an additional pledge?
Simple answer: Additional pledge is not available right now on Shriram Credit LAMF. You cannot add more mutual fund units to an existing loan through the journey at this time.

Customer question: Can I pledge more mutual fund units later?
Simple answer: No. Additional pledge is not possible right now. Once your initial eligible units are pledged during the loan journey, you cannot add more units through MF Central or the online journey at this stage.

Customer question: Can I add more collateral to my LAMF loan?
Simple answer: Additional pledge or adding more mutual fund units as collateral is not available at this time.

Customer question: What if my mutual fund value falls — can I pledge more units?
Simple answer: Additional pledge is not available right now. If your mutual fund value falls and you receive a margin or LTV breach notification, please contact the official Shriram Credit support channel for guidance. Partial repayment may be an option as per your loan terms.

Customer question: Can I change my pledged mutual funds?
Simple answer: Additional pledge is not available at this time. For any other account or pledge-related query, please use the official Shriram Credit support channel.
