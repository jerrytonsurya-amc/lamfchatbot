Question: What is the minimum mutual fund holding required for LAMF?
Simple answer: You need eligible mutual fund units worth approximately Rs. 22,000 if they are equity mutual funds, or approximately Rs. 16,000 if they are debt mutual funds. The exact eligible amount depends on the fund type, applicable LTV (45% for equity, 75% for debt), and the schemes shown in your journey.

Question: What is the minimum portfolio value for LAMF?
Simple answer: Typically around Rs. 22,000 in eligible equity mutual funds or Rs. 16,000 in eligible debt mutual funds, so you can meet the minimum loan amount of Rs. 10,000 after applicable LTV is applied.
