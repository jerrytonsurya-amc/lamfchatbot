SHRIRAM CREDIT — LAMF Minimum Mutual Fund Holding

Customer question: What is the minimum mutual fund holding I need for LAMF?
Simple answer: The minimum mutual fund holding required is between Rs. 15,000 and Rs. 16,000, depending on whether your eligible funds are equity or debt mutual funds. Only eligible schemes shown in your LAMF journey after MF Central portfolio fetch are considered.

Customer question: What is the minimum mutual fund portfolio value for a LAMF loan?
Simple answer: You need eligible mutual fund units worth approximately Rs. 15,000 if they are equity mutual funds, or approximately Rs. 16,000 if they are debt mutual funds. The exact eligible amount depends on the fund type, applicable LTV (65% for equity, 75% for debt), and the schemes shown in your journey.

Customer question: Is there a minimum investment in mutual funds to apply?
Simple answer: Shriram Credit does not require a fixed total portfolio size across all your mutual funds. However, to qualify for the minimum loan amount, you need sufficient eligible mutual fund units — typically around Rs. 15,000 in equity funds or Rs. 16,000 in debt funds, depending on which eligible units you pledge.

Customer question: What is the minimum loan amount for LAMF?
Simple answer: The minimum loan / sanction amount is Rs. 10,000. Your eligible mutual fund holding must be sufficient to support this minimum based on the applicable LTV for equity or debt funds.
