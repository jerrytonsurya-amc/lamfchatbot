SHRIRAM CREDIT — LAMF Loan Tenure

Customer question: What is the repayment tenure for LAMF?
Simple answer: The LAMF loan tenure is 12 months. Both the minimum and maximum loan tenure are 12 months.

Customer question: What loan tenure options are available?
Simple answer: Only a 12-month loan tenure is available for LAMF. The tenure is confirmed at sanction and shown in your Key Fact Statement (KFS) and loan agreement.

Customer question: Can I choose 6, 18, 24, or 36 months tenure?
Simple answer: No. LAMF is offered with a fixed 12-month loan tenure.

Customer question: How does repayment work over the 12-month tenure?
Simple answer: Currently only Balloon repayment is offered. You pay interest only each month and repay the full principal at the end of the 12-month tenure. Your exact schedule is shown in your KFS and loan agreement.
