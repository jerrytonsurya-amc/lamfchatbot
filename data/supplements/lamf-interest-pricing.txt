SHRIRAM CREDIT — LAMF Interest Rate (Dynamic Pricing, Fixed 10.5% at Launch)

Customer question: What is the interest rate for LAMF?
Simple answer: At launch, LAMF is offered at a fixed interest rate of 10.5% per annum. Under SCCL's dynamic pricing model, the applicable rate falls within the range of 9.5% to 15% per annum. Your exact rate is shown in your offer, Key Fact Statement (KFS), and loan agreement.

Customer question: How is my LAMF interest rate calculated?
Simple answer: SCCL uses a dynamic pricing model for LAMF. The interest rate falls within 9.5% to 15% per annum and is determined at origination based on three parameters: your sanctioned limit, your credit score, and the mutual fund type (equity or debt). At launch, a fixed rate of 10.5% per annum applies.

Customer question: Is the LAMF interest rate dynamic?
Simple answer: SCCL follows a dynamic pricing model, which means the rate is set at origination within 9.5% to 15% per annum based on sanctioned limit, credit score, and mutual fund type. Once sanctioned, the rate is fixed for the loan tenure and is not revised mid-tenure. At launch, the rate is fixed at 10.5% per annum.

Customer question: Can my interest rate change during the loan?
Simple answer: No. Once your loan is sanctioned, the interest rate is fixed for the loan tenure and is not revised mid-tenure.

Customer question: How is interest charged?
Simple answer: Interest accrues only on the amount you have withdrawn, not on your full sanctioned limit.

Customer question: What about loans sourced through a partner?
Simple answer: For LSP or partner-sourced loans, the base rate is defined by both the LSP and SCCL in their agreement.

Customer question: Where can I see my interest rate?
Simple answer: Your interest rate is shown in your LAMF journey offer, Key Fact Statement (KFS), and loan agreement before you accept the loan.
