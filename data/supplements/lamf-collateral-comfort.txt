SHRIRAM CREDIT — LAMF Collateral Comfort

Product notes (Collateral Comfort):
- Collateral Comfort shows how comfortably your current loan is supported by the value of your pledged mutual fund investments.
- It is calculated using your latest Loan Outstanding, mutual fund NAV, and applicable LTV limits.
- Hybrid funds are treated as Equity Funds for Collateral Comfort and LTV calculation.
- Collateral Comfort is recalculated dynamically whenever Loan Outstanding or collateral value changes.

Collateral Comfort LTV bands (loan outstanding as a percentage of collateral value, using applicable LTV limits):

Equity Funds (including hybrid funds treated as equity):
- Comfortable: up to 45% — no action required.
- Monitor: above 45% up to 48% — monitor loan and collateral value.
- Action Required: above 48% up to 49% — pledge additional eligible MF units or make a partial repayment.
- Critical: 49% and above — immediate action required. If the required amount is not regularised immediately, your pledged units may be eligible for invocation as per the applicable terms and conditions.

Debt Funds:
- Comfortable: up to 75% — no action required.
- Monitor: above 75% up to 78% — monitor loan and collateral value.
- Action Required: above 78% up to 83% — pledge additional eligible MF units or make a partial repayment.
- Critical: 83% and above — immediate action required. If the required amount is not regularised immediately, your pledged units may be eligible for invocation as per the applicable terms and conditions.

Customer question: What is Collateral Comfort?
Simple answer: Collateral Comfort shows how comfortably your current loan is supported by the value of your pledged mutual fund investments. It uses your latest loan outstanding, mutual fund NAV, and applicable LTV limits (45% for equity and hybrid funds treated as equity, 75% for debt). It is recalculated whenever your loan outstanding or collateral value changes.

Customer question: What are the Collateral Comfort levels?
Simple answer: There are four levels — Comfortable, Monitor, Action Required, and Critical — based on your current LTV versus the applicable limits. For equity (and hybrid treated as equity): Comfortable up to 45%; Monitor above 45% to 48%; Action Required above 48% to 49%; Critical at 49% and above. For debt: Comfortable up to 75%; Monitor above 75% to 78%; Action Required above 78% to 83%; Critical at 83% and above.

Customer question: What does Comfortable Collateral Comfort mean?
Simple answer: Your loan is within the comfortable collateral limit (equity or hybrid as equity: LTV up to 45%; debt: LTV up to 75%). No action is required.

Customer question: What does Monitor Collateral Comfort mean?
Simple answer: Your LTV is approaching the higher limit (equity: above 45% up to 48%; debt: above 75% up to 78%). You should monitor your loan and collateral value.

Customer question: What does Action Required mean for Collateral Comfort?
Simple answer: Your loan has reached a level where corrective action is required (equity: LTV above 48% up to 49%; debt: above 78% up to 83%). You need to either pledge additional eligible mutual fund units through MF Central, or make a partial repayment. You will receive SMS and email with loan outstanding, collateral value, LTV, applicable limit, shortfall amount, and actions to regularise.

Customer question: What does Critical Collateral Comfort mean?
Simple answer: Your loan is at the critical LTV level (equity: 49% or above; debt: 83% or above). Immediate corrective action is required. If the required amount is not regularised immediately, your pledged units may be eligible for invocation as per the applicable terms and conditions.

Customer question: How is my LTV calculated?
Simple answer: LTV = Loan Outstanding ÷ Applicable Collateral Value × 100. The applicable collateral value uses the latest available NAV of your pledged mutual fund units and applicable valuation rules. Collateral Comfort is recalculated dynamically when loan outstanding or collateral value changes.

Customer question: What is the shortfall amount?
Simple answer: The shortfall amount is the amount required to bring your loan back within the Comfortable limit, based on the applicable L1 collateral level: Shortfall Amount = Current Loan Outstanding − Collateral Value supported at the Comfortable limit. If the shortfall is positive, you need corrective action — either pledge additional eligible units through MF Central or make a partial repayment.

Customer question: What happens when Action Required is triggered?
Simple answer: You receive SMS and email showing current loan outstanding, collateral value, current LTV, applicable LTV limit, shortfall amount, and action required. You can regularise by pledging additional eligible mutual fund units through MF Central or by making a partial repayment. Collateral Comfort is then recalculated using the latest values.

Customer question: What happens if LTV reaches the Critical level?
Simple answer: For equity funds, critical is 49% and above; for debt funds, 83% and above. Immediate corrective action is required. If the required amount is not regularised immediately, your pledged units may be eligible for invocation as per the applicable terms and conditions. Invocation may also be initiated if you have defaulted on 2 consecutive EMIs, or if an LTV breach is not regularised.

Customer question: What is invocation?
Simple answer: Invocation means Shriram Credit (SCCL) redeems the pledged mutual fund units through MF Central to recover the outstanding loan dues.

Customer question: How does invoke and revoke work?
Simple answer: Invoke and Revoke are disabled while the UTR for a pending disbursement is awaited. If invocation is initiated, debt fund units are considered first, then equity fund units; within each category, units are in alphabetical order by fund name. Any invoke or revoke request not approved is automatically cancelled at 8:00 PM on the same day. If the loan is active and invoke is initiated, Rs. 100 plus GST lien removal charges are posted to the backend. If the loan is closed and revoke is initiated, no additional lien removal charges are posted as those were collected upfront.

Customer question: Are hybrid mutual funds treated as equity for LTV?
Simple answer: Yes. Hybrid funds are treated as equity funds for Collateral Comfort and LTV calculation (45% comfortable limit applies).
