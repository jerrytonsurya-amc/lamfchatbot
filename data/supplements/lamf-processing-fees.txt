SHRIRAM CREDIT — LAMF Processing Fee and Other Charges

Customer question: What is the processing fee for LAMF?
Simple answer: Processing fee is based on your sanctioned limit and is deducted upfront from disbursement:
- Up to 10 lakh: maximum 1% of sanctioned limit or Rs. 999 plus GST, whichever is higher
- 10 lakh to 50 lakh: 0.8% of sanctioned limit
- More than 50 lakh: 0.5% of sanctioned limit

Customer question: What is the minimum processing fee?
Simple answer: For sanctioned amounts up to 10 lakh, the minimum processing fee is Rs. 999 plus GST (or 1% of the sanctioned limit, whichever is higher).

Customer question: Are stamp duty and lien charges included in the processing fee?
Simple answer: No. Stamp duty, lien marking charges of Rs. 450 plus GST, and lien release charges of Rs. 100 plus GST are exclusive of the processing fee — they are charged separately. You can find the exact details regarding your specific loan charges in your Key Fact Statement (KFS) and loan agreement.

Customer question: What other upfront charges apply besides processing fee?
Simple answer: In addition to the processing fee, separate charges may apply including stamp duty (as per applicable state law), lien marking charges of Rs. 450 plus GST, and lien release charges of Rs. 100 plus GST. These charges are exclusive of the processing fee. Applicable amounts are deducted upfront at disbursement where relevant and are disclosed in your KFS and loan agreement.

Customer question: Where can I see my exact loan charges?
Simple answer: You can find the exact details regarding your specific loan charges in your Key Fact Statement (KFS) and loan agreement.
