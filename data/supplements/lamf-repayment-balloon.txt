SHRIRAM CREDIT — LAMF Repayment (Balloon — Launch Offering)

Customer question: What are the repayment options for a LAMF loan?
Simple answer: Shriram Credit is starting with balloon repayment only. You pay monthly interest during the loan tenure, and the principal is repaid at the end of the tenure.

Customer question: What is balloon repayment?
Simple answer: Under balloon repayment, you pay interest only each month during the loan period. The full principal amount is due at the end of the agreed tenure — not in monthly principal instalments like a normal EMI.

Customer question: Is EMI repayment available?
Simple answer: At launch, LAMF is offered with balloon repayment only (interest-only monthly payments, principal at end of tenure). Standard EMI repayment is not available at this stage.

Customer question: When is my interest rate and repayment structure fixed?
Simple answer: Your interest rate, tenure, and repayment terms are determined and shown at the time your loan is sanctioned — in your Key Fact Statement (KFS) and loan agreement. Once disbursed, the interest rate does not change for the duration of your loan.

Customer question: Can repayment terms change later?
Simple answer: Product features and offerings may be updated from time to time for future customers. For your loan, the repayment type, interest rate, tenure, and charges applicable to you are those confirmed at sanction and shown in your KFS and agreement.

Customer question: How will monthly payments be collected?
Simple answer: Monthly interest is collected automatically from your verified bank account through the e-Mandate you set up during the journey. Please ensure sufficient balance on the due date.

Customer question: What happens at the end of the tenure?
Simple answer: At the end of the agreed tenure, you repay the outstanding principal amount as per your loan agreement and repayment schedule.
