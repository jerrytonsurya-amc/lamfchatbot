SHRIRAM CREDIT — LAMF Foreclosure / Pre-closure

Customer question: What is the foreclosure process for LAMF?
Simple answer: You can repay your outstanding loan amount before the end of the agreed tenure through foreclosure or pre-closure. Shriram Credit LAMF does not charge any foreclosure or pre-closure fees.

Customer question: Are there pre-closure or foreclosure charges?
Simple answer: No. There are no foreclosure or pre-closure charges on LAMF — whether you close within 3 months of your first withdrawal or later.

Customer question: Can I repay the loan early?
Simple answer: Yes. Early repayment or foreclosure is allowed as per your loan terms. There are no foreclosure or pre-closure charges. Please check your loan agreement and Key Fact Statement (KFS) for the repayment process.

Customer question: Is there a 3% charge if I close the loan within 3 months?
Simple answer: No. Shriram Credit LAMF does not charge any foreclosure or pre-closure fees, including within the first 3 months after withdrawal.

Customer question: Are there charges for part payment before maturity?
Simple answer: No foreclosure or pre-closure charges apply on part payment or full early closure. Please refer to your KFS and loan agreement for the repayment process.
