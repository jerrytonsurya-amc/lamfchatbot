SHRIRAM CREDIT — LAMF Pre-closure / Foreclosure and Loan Closure

Customer question: Are there pre-closure or foreclosure charges?
Simple answer: No. Pre-closure charges are nil. You can close your loan early at any time without a pre-closure charge.

Customer question: Is there a charge if I close the loan within 3 months?
Simple answer: No. There is no pre-closure charge, whether you close the loan within 3 months of your first withdrawal or later.

Customer question: Can I repay the loan early?
Simple answer: Yes. You can pre-close the loan at any time and there are no pre-closure charges. Please check your Key Fact Statement (KFS) and loan agreement.

Customer question: Are there charges for part payment before maturity?
Simple answer: No. There are no charges for part payment.

Customer question: What happens when I pre-close my loan?
Simple answer: There is no charge. Your pledge is released and the account is closed.

Customer question: What happens at maturity?
Simple answer: When all instalments are paid through the tenure, there is no charge. The account is closed at maturity, the pledge is released, and any excess amount is refunded.

Customer question: What is the cooling-off period?
Simple answer: You can exit the loan within the cooling-off period of 3 days from the disbursement date. There is no interest and no closure charge for the cooling-off period, but upfront charges already collected are not refunded.
